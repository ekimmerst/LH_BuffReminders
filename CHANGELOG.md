# Changelog

## 1.3.4 - Forever and Retail

- Prevent false missing-buff alerts when an active aura, such as Mark of the Wild, is hidden by combat restrictions.
- Build the centered combat layout from buffs with a known missing state.

## 1.3.3 - Forever and Retail

- Keep visible reminder icons adjacent and centered as buffs, forms, and cooldowns change during combat.
- Make reminder icons display-only. The unlocked position preview remains draggable.

## 1.3.2 - Forever and Retail

- Show reminders only while their spells are usable, including form and resource requirements.
- Use the readable cooldown activity flag when cooldown times are hidden in combat.

## 1.3.1 - Forever and Retail

- Wait until a buff spell's cooldown finishes before showing its reminder. Available charges count as ready.

## 1.3.0 - Forever and Retail

- Add a character settings source for reminder position and lock state.
- Save monitored buffs, watch choices, and exclusions per character, migrating existing class settings on first load.

## 1.2.1 - Forever

- Pulse newly shown missing-buff icons to draw attention.
